About Daegon Charts

Independent weekly music charts powered by publicly available data. No label influence, no editorial bias — just numbers.

How It Works

Every week, data is collected from public sources — primarily streaming platform counts, sales figures, and radio airplay metrics. This data is processed through weighted formulas to produce each chart ranking.

All weekly charts are generated by Google Apps Script codes running directly in Google Sheets. Each chart has its own spreadsheet that is updated automatically via these scripts. The site reads from public Google Sheets links, which allows the data to be displayed here in real time.

Charts are updated weekly and dated accordingly. Historical data is preserved so you can track how songs, albums, and artists perform over time.

Auditing & Transparency

All chart data is sourced from publicly available APIs and published reports. Streaming data comes directly from streaming platform APIs. Sales data is compiled from public sales reports. Radio airplay data is sourced from monitored station logs.

Every chart entry is auditable by design. Historical records are reviewed and corrected when better source information, duplicate credits, or continuity issues are identified.

Weekly Charts

Each weekly chart uses a specific formula to rank entries. Here's how each one works:

Hot 100Sales (7d) × 3 + Streaming (28d) × 2 + Airplay × 1 = Points

The flagship chart combines sales, streaming and radio activity into one weekly ranking.

Artist 50Sales (7d) + Streaming (28d) ÷ 2 = Units

Ranks artists by total consumption units across their catalog.

Top 100 AlbumsSales (7d) + Streaming (28d) ÷ 2 = Units

Albums are ranked by consumption units, with album editions normalized into a single entry.

Digital Songs SalesSales (7d) × 1,000

Sales-focused ranking based on the current weekly window.

Streaming SongsStreaming (rolling 4 weeks) × 1,000

Streaming chart based on a rolling four-week window.

Top Albums SalesSales (7d) × 1,000

Sales-only album ranking.

Top Streaming AlbumsStreaming (28d) × 1,000

Streaming-only album ranking over the last 28 days.

Radio Songs(Previous Audience × 0.90) + (Weekly Spins × 10) = Audience

Ranks songs by accumulated radio audience with weekly decay.

Year-End Charts (YEC)

Year-End charts are cumulative rankings that cover an entire calendar year. They aggregate weekly performance to determine the most successful songs, albums, and artists of each year.

Greatest of All Time (GOAT)

The GOAT charts rank the biggest hits across the complete chart history using cumulative performance data across all available weeks.

Stats
