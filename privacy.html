Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

Daegon Charts is an independent music-chart website. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the site and how third-party services may be involved.

Information stored by the site

The site may use browser storage for functional preferences such as theme choice and cached artwork references. This information is stored in your browser to improve repeat visits and is not intended to identify you personally.

Technical data

Like most websites, the hosting platform and connected infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver the site, such as IP address, browser type, device information, request time and error logs. These records may be used for security, reliability and performance.

Third-party services

Daegon Charts may retrieve public music metadata, artwork or previews from third-party services. When your browser connects directly to such a service, that provider may receive standard technical request information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising and Google AdSense

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at Google's partner-sites policy page.

Analytics

The site may use traffic or performance analytics in the future to understand aggregate usage, identify errors and improve navigation. If analytics are enabled, this policy will be updated as needed and consent controls will be added where legally required.

Cookies and consent

Functional cookies or local storage may be necessary for preferences and site operation. Advertising or analytics cookies, when enabled, may require consent depending on the visitor's jurisdiction. Daegon Charts will present consent controls where legally required.

External links

This site may contain links to third-party websites. Daegon Charts is not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when services, advertising tools or legal requirements change. The revision date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

Questions

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the contact page.
